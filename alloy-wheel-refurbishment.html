What we do

Alloy wheel restoration properly done.

Kerb damage repair

The most common type of alloy damage — scrapes and gouges from kerbs and tight parking spaces. We strip the wheel, fill and sand the damaged area to achieve a smooth surface, then repaint and lacquer to match the original finish. Virtually invisible when done properly.

Corrosion removal

Alloy wheels corrode over time — particularly on older vehicles or those exposed to road salt. Corrosion causes the paint and lacquer to bubble and peel. We strip the wheel to bare metal, treat the corrosion and refinish from scratch for a lasting result.

Full refurbishment

All four wheels stripped, repaired where necessary, painted and lacquered in the original colour — or a new colour of your choice. The most cost-effective way to transform the look of a vehicle. A set of refurbished alloys makes a significant difference to a car's overall appearance and value.

Colour changes and custom finishes

Want to change your alloys from silver to gloss black, or try a diamond-cut effect? We can respray alloys to any colour — standard colours or custom mixed. We advise on what will suit the vehicle and provide samples before proceeding.

SMART alloy repair

For single-wheel kerb damage where the rest of the wheel is in good condition, we can carry out a SMART (Small to Medium Area Repair Technology) repair — a targeted fix rather than a full strip and respray. Faster and less expensive for minor damage.

Pricing

Single wheel SMART repair from around £60–£80. Full four-wheel refurbishment priced on inspection. Send us photos of your wheels for an accurate quote — no obligation.
