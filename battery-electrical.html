Common faults

Battery, alternator or starter — we'll tell you which.

These three components work together. A fault in any one of them can cause similar symptoms — proper diagnosis before replacement is essential.

Battery replacement

A car battery typically lasts 3 to 5 years. Signs of a failing battery include slow engine cranking, the battery warning light, electrical components behaving erratically and a car that needs jump-starting regularly. We test your battery properly before recommending replacement — not every flat battery needs replacing.

Alternator replacement

The alternator charges the battery while the engine runs. A failing alternator means the battery drains while driving. Signs include a battery warning light, dimming headlights or interior lights at idle, and a car that starts fine but then won't restart after a short journey. We test alternator output before replacing.

Starter motor replacement

A failing starter motor often gives a clicking sound when you turn the key, or nothing at all despite a fully charged battery. It can also cause intermittent starting problems — working sometimes and not others. We test the starter circuit fully to confirm the fault before replacing the unit.

Why diagnosis matters

A flat battery is often blamed on the battery itself — but the cause may be a failing alternator that's no longer keeping it charged, or a parasitic drain caused by a faulty component. Replacing a battery without identifying the root cause means the new one will fail in the same way. We test the full charging system before recommending any replacement.

Stop-start systems

Vehicles with stop-start technology use AGM or EFB batteries rather than standard lead-acid batteries. Fitting the wrong battery type damages the stop-start system and the battery management system. We supply the correct specification battery for your vehicle every time.
