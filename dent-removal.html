Two methods — right tool for the job

Paintless dent removal vs traditional repair.

Paintless dent removal (PDR)

PDR uses specialist tools to massage the dent out from behind the panel — restoring the original shape without any filler, primer or paint. No damage to your original factory finish. Suitable for dents where the paint is undamaged and the metal hasn't creased or cracked. Faster, less expensive and preserves the original paintwork.

Traditional dent repair

Where the paint is cracked, the metal has creased, or the dent is too severe for PDR, traditional repair involves panel beating, filler where necessary, and a full respray of the affected panel with colour-matched paint. Achieves a factory-quality finish on more significant damage.

Which method is right for your dent?

We assess every dent individually and recommend the most appropriate method. PDR is usually preferable where possible — it's faster and less expensive. We won't recommend a full panel respray if PDR will achieve the same result.

Car park dents and door dings

The most common type of dent — a door from the next car, a trolley, a post. Usually small but visible and frustrating. Most car park dents are ideal candidates for PDR and can be completed in a matter of hours.

Hail damage

A hailstorm can leave dozens of small dents across bonnet, roof and boot. PDR is the standard treatment for hail damage — effective on multiple small dents without the cost of repainting every affected panel.

Pricing

Dent removal pricing depends on size, location and method. Small PDR repairs start from around £60–£100. Send us photos of the damage for an accurate quote — no obligation.
