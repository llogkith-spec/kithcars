Warning signs

How to tell if your head gasket has failed.

Caught early it is fixable. Left too long, it destroys the engine.

White smoke from exhaust

Coolant burning in the combustion chamber produces distinctive white smoke — particularly on start-up or under load. One of the clearest head gasket signs.

Overheating

A blown head gasket allows combustion gases into the cooling system, preventing it working correctly. If the temperature gauge is high, stop immediately.

Milky oil

Coolant mixing with engine oil produces a milky residue on the oil filler cap or dipstick. Contaminated oil loses its lubricating properties rapidly.

Coolant loss without visible leak

Regularly topping up coolant but cannot find a leak? It may be burning through the head gasket into the combustion chamber.

Bubbling coolant reservoir

Combustion gases entering the cooling system cause bubbling in the coolant reservoir. Confirmed with a combustion gas test.

Loss of power

Head gasket failure reduces compression in the affected cylinder, causing misfires, rough running and loss of power.
