What is a SMART repair?

Small to Medium Area Repair Technology — a targeted repair process for minor bodywork damage. Treating only the affected area rather than respraying an entire panel.

Bumper scuffs and scrapes

Plastic bumper scuffs from car parks, kerbs and minor impacts. We reshape, fill where necessary, colour-match and lacquer. Completed in hours in most cases.

Minor scratches and paint chips

Surface scratches, light scuffs and stone chip clusters. Cleaned, primed, colour-matched and lacquered for a seamless finish — much less expensive than a full panel respray.

Small dent repairs

Car park dents too small to justify a full panel repair. Where paint is intact, PDR is used first. Where paint damage is involved, SMART techniques restore both shape and finish.

When is SMART repair appropriate?

Best for damage covering less than a third of a panel where surrounding paintwork is in reasonable condition. For more extensive damage, a full panel respray gives a better result — we'll advise honestly.
