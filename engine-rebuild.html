Complex engine work

When most garages say no, we say let's look properly.

Full engine strip-down and rebuild

We strip the engine completely, assess every component, replace all worn or damaged parts and rebuild to specification. This includes new bearings, seals, gaskets, piston rings and any components found to be beyond tolerance. A rebuilt engine done properly outlasts a second-hand replacement.

Reconditioned engine supply and fit

Where a full rebuild isn't the most cost-effective route, we can source a quality reconditioned engine and supply and fit it. We only use reputable suppliers with proper warranties — not unverified second-hand units with unknown history.

What causes engines to need rebuilding?

Common causes include oil starvation (low oil pressure, blocked oil ways), overheating damage, timing chain or belt failure causing valve and piston contact, hydrolocking (water ingestion), and general wear over high mileage. We diagnose the root cause before recommending a rebuild — to make sure the same fault doesn't destroy the rebuilt engine.

Honest assessment first

Not every engine that sounds bad needs a full rebuild. We carry out a thorough assessment — compression test, leak-down test, full diagnostic — and give you an honest view of the options and costs before any work starts. Sometimes a targeted repair is all that's needed.

All makes and models

We rebuild engines across all makes including BMW, Audi, Mercedes, Volkswagen, Ford, Vauxhall, Toyota, Nissan and more. Our team has the experience to work on both common and complex engine configurations.

Pricing

Engine rebuild costs vary significantly depending on the engine, the extent of damage and parts required. We provide a full written assessment and quote before any work begins. Call us or send a message to discuss your specific vehicle.
