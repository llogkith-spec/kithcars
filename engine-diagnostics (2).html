Accurate diagnosis first

Fix the right thing first time.

A warning light on its own tells you nothing. The ECU fault code tells you everything. We read it properly and give you a straight answer.

What does a diagnostic involve?

We connect professional-grade diagnostic equipment to your vehicle's OBD port and read all stored fault codes from the ECU — including live data, pending faults and manufacturer-specific codes.

What warning lights do we cover?

Engine management, DPF, glow plug, oil pressure, EML, ABS, airbag, transmission and more. All warning lights across all vehicle systems — not just engine faults.

What happens after?

We explain fault codes in plain English, tell you what they mean and give a clear written quote for the repair. If you proceed, the £80+VAT diagnostic fee is deducted from your repair bill.

Can you just clear the light?

We can — but we won't without identifying why it came on. Clearing a fault code without fixing the underlying issue means the light returns, often with additional damage.

How much does it cost?

Diagnostics from £80+VAT, deducted from your repair bill if you proceed. Transparent pricing throughout — no inflated repair quotes after a diagnostic.
