Common exhaust faults

Exhaust problems we fix.

Blowing exhaust

A blowing exhaust — a loud, irregular roaring or hissing from underneath the vehicle — is usually caused by a hole, crack or failed joint in the exhaust system. Often the manifold gasket, a flexi pipe or a joint between sections. We locate the blow and repair or replace the affected section.

Exhaust rattle

A rattling or droning noise, particularly at certain engine speeds, is usually caused by a loose or broken exhaust mount, a broken internal baffle in the silencer, or a loose heat shield. We identify the source and fix it — often a quick and inexpensive repair.

MOT exhaust failure

Exhaust emissions and exhaust system condition are both checked at MOT. A failed emissions test, excessive smoke or a visibly damaged exhaust system will cause a failure. We carry out exhaust repairs to get your vehicle through — and can rebook the MOT re-test immediately.

Catalytic converter

A failed catalytic converter causes increased emissions, reduced performance and a rotten egg smell from the exhaust. Cat replacement is required for emissions compliance. We supply and fit quality replacement cats for all makes and models.

Full exhaust replacement

Where repair isn't viable — heavily corroded systems or multiple failure points — we supply and fit a replacement exhaust system. We use quality systems and can advise on standard replacement or upgraded options where applicable.

Pricing

Exhaust repairs range from a straightforward mount replacement (from around £30) to a full system replacement (varies by vehicle). We inspect and quote accurately before any work starts — no guesswork, no surprises.
