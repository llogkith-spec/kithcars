What is included

What each tier covers.

Preparation is what separates a lasting respray from one that starts peeling within a year.

Budget finish

Solid colour resprays with standard preparation. Panels cleaned, flatted and primed. Existing paintwork not fully stripped. Good value for vehicles where cosmetic appearance matters but not perfection.

Standard finish

Metallic and solid colours. Thorough panel preparation. All door shuts, boot and bonnet edges painted to match. Most customers choose this option for a clean, consistent result.

Premium finish

Full strip-down where possible. Engine bay painted. All jambs, shuts and internal panels painted. Full colour change option. The most thorough and longest-lasting finish available.

Rust treatment included

Any rust found during preparation is treated before painting begins. We will not paint over rust — it would fail within months.

Colour matching

We match your chosen colour precisely — whether retaining the original colour or changing to something new. Colour samples provided before work starts.

Workmanship warranty

All respray work carries a workmanship warranty. Quality automotive paint systems used throughout.
