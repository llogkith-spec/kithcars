Surface to deep

What type of scratch do you have?

Surface scratches and scuffs

Scratches that only affect the lacquer or clear coat — they catch the light but you can't feel them with your fingernail. These are the most repairable type of scratch, often addressed through machine polishing and paint correction without any respraying needed.

Paint layer scratches

Scratches that penetrate through the lacquer into the paint layer — visible as a white or coloured line depending on the depth. These require touch-up painting with colour-matched paint, followed by blending and lacquering for a seamless repair.

Deep scratches to bare metal

Scratches that go through all paint layers to bare metal need treatment quickly — bare metal corrodes. We clean, prime, paint and lacquer the affected area. For key scratches across a panel, a full panel respray may give a better result than touch-up repairs.

Paint chips

Stone chips on the bonnet and front bumper are common on any vehicle used on motorways. Small chips treated quickly prevent rust from taking hold. We carry out precision touch-up repairs using computerised colour-matched paint.

Computerised colour matching

Every scratch repair uses our computerised colour matching system to mix paint precisely to your vehicle's colour code — accounting for age and fade. A repair that doesn't match the surrounding paint is worse than the original scratch.
