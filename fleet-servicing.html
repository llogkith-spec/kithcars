Built for businesses

Fleet support that keeps your vehicles earning.

Scheduled fleet maintenance

We build a maintenance schedule around your fleet's requirements — service intervals, MOT dates, tyre checks and seasonal inspections. Proactive maintenance reduces unexpected breakdowns and keeps running costs predictable. We remind you when vehicles are due and book them in around your operational needs.

Priority scheduling

Fleet customers receive priority booking — same-day and next-day appointments available for urgent work. We understand that a van off the road is a job not getting done. We work to your timeframe, not ours.

Weekly invoicing

Approved business accounts receive weekly consolidated invoices — one document covering all vehicles for the week, making maintenance costs simple to manage and reconcile. Flexible payment terms available for established accounts.

Van and commercial vehicle servicing

We service all makes of van and light commercial vehicle — Ford Transit, Mercedes Sprinter, Vauxhall Vivaro, VW Transporter, Renault Trafic, Peugeot Boxer and more. MOT, servicing, brakes, clutch, electrical and bodywork all handled in-house.

Taxi and private hire servicing

We understand the compliance requirements for licensed vehicles. Regular servicing, PCO-standard checks and fast turnaround to minimise time off the road. We work around your shifts where possible.

Setting up a fleet account

Contact us to discuss your fleet size, vehicle types and servicing requirements. We'll put together a maintenance plan and pricing structure that works for your business. No minimum fleet size — we work with sole traders through to larger operations.
