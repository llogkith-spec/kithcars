Manufacturer-Level Diagnostics

We use diagnostic equipment compatible with BMW, Audi, Mercedes and VW manufacturer systems — reading all fault codes across all vehicle systems, not just the engine. VCDS for VAG group, ISTA for BMW, Mercedes XENTRY-compatible.

Correct Specification Parts

German manufacturers require specific parts to maintain vehicle integrity. We source quality OEM-equivalent parts meeting manufacturer specifications — not generic substitutes.

Correct Oil Specification

BMW N-series engines require 5W-30 C3 oil. Mercedes and many Audi/VW engines require 5W-30 or 5W-40 to specific standards (MB 229.5, VW 507.00 etc.). We always use the correct specification for your vehicle.

Audi & VW Specialist

DSG gearbox service, timing chain/belt replacement, DPF cleaning, EGR faults, cam follower inspection (2.0 TDI), pump-injector (PD) engine servicing. Full Audi and VW coverage across all models.

Mercedes Specialist

Full Mercedes servicing, A-service and B-service, STAR diagnostic reads, air suspension repair, automatic transmission service, and all mechanical repairs across C, E, S, GLE, GLC and Sprinter.

Porsche — also welcome

We service and repair Porsche vehicles. Cayenne, Macan, Panamera and 911. Porsche requires specific parts and specific diagnostic protocols — we have both.
