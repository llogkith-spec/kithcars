Warning signs

Signs your brakes need attention.

Squealing or grinding noise

A high-pitched squeal when braking usually means the brake pad wear indicators are making contact with the disc — a built-in warning that pads are near the end of their life. A grinding metal-on-metal sound means the pads are completely worn and the calliper is contacting the disc directly. Book in immediately.

Pulling to one side

If the car pulls left or right under braking, one brake is applying more force than the other. Common causes include a seized calliper, uneven pad wear, or a sticking brake hose. Left unaddressed this becomes a safety issue.

Pulsating or vibrating pedal

A pulsating brake pedal or vibration through the steering wheel under braking usually indicates warped brake discs. This happens when discs overheat and distort — common after heavy use or repeated emergency stops.

Soft or spongy pedal

A pedal that travels further than normal before the brakes engage, or feels spongy rather than firm, usually indicates air in the brake lines or a brake fluid issue. This requires immediate attention — brake fluid absorbs moisture over time and should be replaced every two years.

What we replace

Brake pads and discs (axle pairs), brake callipers (where seized or damaged), brake hoses, brake fluid flush and bleed. We always replace pads and discs together on the same axle — replacing pads on worn discs or vice versa causes uneven braking and accelerated wear.

Pricing
