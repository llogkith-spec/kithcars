Which service does your vehicle need?

We follow manufacturer schedules for every make and model — and include a full written inspection report with every service.

Interim service — from £93

Recommended every 6 months or 6,000 miles. Engine oil and filter change, 30-point safety inspection covering brakes, tyres, lights, fluid levels, belts and more.

Oil & filter service — from £131

Engine oil and filter change — the fundamental service item that keeps your engine protected. Suitable for newer vehicles on extended intervals.

Full service — from £141

Recommended annually or every 12,000 miles. Everything in the interim service plus air filter, cabin filter, spark plug inspection, fuel filter check and a 60-point inspection.

Major service — from £176

The most comprehensive service. Includes spark plug replacement, cambelt condition check, coolant flush and full system inspection. Recommended every 2 years or 24,000 miles.

Manufacturer schedule servicing

We follow the manufacturer's schedule for your specific vehicle — BMW, Audi, Mercedes, Ford, Vauxhall or any other make. Quality parts, full service history documentation.

Full written inspection report

Every service includes a written inspection report — green for pass, amber for advisory, red for immediate attention. You know exactly what condition your vehicle is in.
