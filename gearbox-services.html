Manual Gearbox Oil Change

Old degraded gearbox oil causes wear. We drain and refill with the correct specification. Recommended every 40,000-60,000 miles.

Automatic Transmission Service

Fluid and filter change for all automatic types including traditional auto, CVT and DSG. Degraded ATF causes harsh shifting and eventual failure.

Gearbox Fault Diagnosis (£122+VAT)

Warning light, slipping, harsh shifts or unusual noises — deep diagnostic to identify gearbox faults accurately before recommending any repair.

Clutch vs Gearbox Diagnosis

Many apparent gearbox faults are actually clutch or hydraulic issues. We diagnose the correct system before any work starts.

Gearbox Removal & Replacement

Where repair is not viable, full gearbox removal and replacement. All approved by our senior technician before parts are ordered.
