Complete headlamp unit replacement

Where the headlamp assembly is cracked, broken or physically damaged, we source and fit a replacement unit. We use quality OEM-equivalent units to ensure correct beam pattern and alignment.

MOT failure — headlamp faults

Headlamp alignment, cracked lenses and non-functioning units are all MOT failure points. We carry out headlamp repairs and replacements and can retest immediately.

Headlamp levelling and alignment

After fitting a replacement headlamp unit, correct alignment is essential — incorrectly aimed headlamps dazzle other drivers and reduce your visibility. We set beam alignment correctly on every headlamp replacement.

HID / Xenon headlamp units

HID and xenon headlamp units require specialist handling due to the high voltages involved. We replace HID units and ballasts on all vehicles — including BMW, Mercedes, Audi and VW.

LED and matrix headlamp units

Modern LED and matrix headlamp assemblies are significantly more expensive than conventional units. We source competitive pricing on quality replacements for all LED headlamp systems.

Headlamp restoration

Where the headlamp lens is yellowed or hazy but the unit is otherwise undamaged, restoration is possible — from £40 per unit. Often more cost-effective than full unit replacement.
