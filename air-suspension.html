Common air suspension faults

Air bag, compressor and system faults.

Air suspension offers a superior ride when working correctly — but requires specialist diagnosis when it fails.

Vehicle sitting low

A vehicle sitting noticeably lower on one corner or overall is usually caused by a failed or leaking air bag. The air bag deflates, removing the ride height support on that corner.

Suspension warning light

Most air suspension systems have a dedicated warning light. When illuminated, a diagnostic check identifies the specific fault — whether air bag, compressor, ride height sensor or control module.

Compressor running constantly

The compressor should only run occasionally to maintain correct pressure. If it runs continuously, it indicates a leak in the system — usually an air bag, pipe or connection. A compressor run to failure is expensive to replace.

Air bag replacement

We source and fit quality replacement air bags for all vehicles with air suspension — Land Rover, BMW, Mercedes, Audi, Volkswagen and more.

Compressor replacement

Where the compressor has failed — often due to overwork caused by a leaking air bag — we replace the unit and rectify the underlying leak to prevent the replacement failing in the same way.

Ride height sensor and module faults

Ride height sensors and the suspension control module can also cause air suspension faults. We carry out a full diagnostic check before recommending any parts.
