Warning Signs

Is your timing chain wearing out?

Unlike a timing belt, chains are designed to last the life of the engine — but high mileage, poor servicing, and low oil levels cause them to wear. Watch for:

  • Rattling or clattering noise on startup that fades after a few seconds
  • Check engine light on (timing-related fault codes)
  • Engine misfiring or running rough
  • Loss of power or sluggish acceleration
  • Oil pressure warning light (oil feeds the chain tensioner)

What happens if you ignore it?

A worn timing chain can jump a tooth or snap without warning, causing the valves and pistons to collide. That's catastrophic engine damage — a full engine replacement averages over £3,500. Replacing the chain early costs a fraction of that.
