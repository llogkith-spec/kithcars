Fluid services

What we change and why.

Vehicle fluids degrade over time — degraded fluids cause component wear, corrosion and safety issues.

Brake Fluid Change

Brake fluid is hygroscopic — it absorbs moisture from the air over time, lowering its boiling point. Old brake fluid can boil under hard braking, causing brake fade. Recommended every 2 years or as per manufacturer schedule.

Coolant Flush

Old coolant loses its anti-corrosion and anti-freeze properties. We drain and refill with the correct coolant specification for your vehicle. Recommended every 2–4 years depending on manufacturer.

Power Steering Fluid

Power steering fluid degrades over time and can cause seal failure and pump wear. Signs: whining noise from the power steering pump, heavy or jerky steering.

Gearbox Oil / Transmission Fluid

Manual gearbox oil should be changed every 40,000–60,000 miles. Automatic transmission fluid every 40,000–60,000 miles or as specified. Degraded fluid causes harsh shifting and gearbox wear.

Engine Flush

A pre-service engine flush removes sludge and deposits from the engine before the new oil goes in. Recommended on high-mileage vehicles or those with an unknown service history.

All fluids to manufacturer specification

We always use the correct fluid specification for your vehicle — not a universal substitute. BMW, Mercedes, Audi and VW vehicles require specific fluids that matter.
