What we offer

Panel respray to full colour change.

Preparation determines quality. We take the time to prepare surfaces properly — the finish lasts.

Single panel respray

For accident damage, deep scratches or corrosion on a single panel — bumper, door, wing, bonnet or boot lid. Prepared, primed, colour-matched and lacquered to match surrounding bodywork.

Partial respray

Multiple panels or a section of the vehicle — for example, a full front end following an accident. Panels blended carefully for colour consistency across the affected area.

Full respray

Complete removal of existing paint and a full respray from bare metal — for heavily corroded bodywork, accident-damaged vehicles or a full colour change. The most thorough and longest-lasting finish.

Colour change respray

Want a different colour? We can respray to any colour including custom mixed colours. Full colour changes include jambs, door shuts, engine bay and boot for a complete, consistent result.

Preparation is everything

The quality of a respray is determined by preparation, not just the paint. We flat, prime and treat any corrosion before a single coat of colour is applied. Shortcuts in prep always show.

Pricing

Respray pricing depends on panels, vehicle size and preparation required. Detailed written quote after inspecting the vehicle. Send photos for a ballpark estimate.
