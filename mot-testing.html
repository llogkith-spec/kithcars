What to expect

Straightforward MOT, honest results.

Fixed price — £54.85

The DVSA sets a maximum fee of £54.85 for a car MOT. That's what we charge — no add-ons, no surprises. We never inflate the price or add admin fees.

Pre-MOT inspection

Not sure if your vehicle will pass? Book a pre-MOT inspection first. We check all the items the tester will look at and advise you on anything that needs attention before the official test. Avoids the cost and inconvenience of a failure and re-test.

Same-day certificate

Pass or fail, we issue your result the same day. Certificates are issued digitally through the DVSA system and accessible immediately via the government's vehicle enquiry service.

Immediate repairs if needed

If your vehicle fails, we can carry out all required repairs immediately — getting you back on the road the same day in most cases. No need to take it elsewhere or rebook.

Combine with a service

Booking a service alongside your MOT is the most cost-effective way to keep your vehicle compliant. We offer interim, full and major services and can combine them with your MOT booking — saving you a return visit.

All vehicles welcome

Cars, vans and light commercial vehicles up to 3,000kg. All makes and models. Book online or call us to confirm your slot.
