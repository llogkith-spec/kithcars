How to identify a failing wheel bearing.

Wheel bearing noise can be confused with tyre noise. These signs point specifically to a bearing.

Rumbling or growling noise

A continuous rumbling that changes pitch with speed — getting louder as you speed up. Often more pronounced at motorway speeds.

Noise changes when cornering

If the noise changes when you turn — louder on one side — it points to a specific corner bearing.

Vibration through steering

Advanced bearing wear can cause vibration through the steering wheel, particularly at motorway speeds.

MOT failure item

Excessive wheel bearing play is an MOT failure point. We can carry out the replacement and retest.

Which corner?

We diagnose which corner is affected before replacing anything — not all four just in case.
