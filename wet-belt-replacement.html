Specialist knowledge required

Wet belt systems need a specialist — not a general garage.

What is a wet belt?

A wet belt (also called a timing belt in oil, or TBIO) is a timing belt that runs inside the engine immersed in engine oil, rather than in a dry housing. The design was intended to reduce friction and extend belt life — but it requires the oil to be kept clean and changed regularly. Neglected oil is the primary cause of wet belt failure.

Which engines use wet belt systems?

Wet belt systems are used on Ford 1.0 EcoBoost engines (found in the Fiesta, Focus, EcoSport and others), Renault and Nissan engines sharing the same architecture, and several other modern small-capacity engines. If you own a Ford with a 1.0 EcoBoost engine, this applies to your vehicle.

What goes wrong?

Contaminated or degraded oil causes the belt to deteriorate prematurely. Metal particles in old oil act as an abrasive, wearing the belt rapidly. In severe cases the belt stretches, skips a tooth, or snaps entirely — causing the same catastrophic engine damage as a failed conventional cambelt. Bent valves and damaged pistons are common outcomes.

Warning signs

A rattling or ticking noise from the engine, particularly on startup, is the most common warning sign. Rough running, misfires and a check engine light can also indicate wet belt issues. Because the belt runs in oil, visual inspection is not possible without disassembly — diagnosis requires opening the engine.

What's included in the replacement?

We replace the full wet belt kit — belt, tensioner, idler and associated seals. A fresh oil and filter change is mandatory as part of the job. We also inspect the oil pump and associated components for wear. Everything is done to manufacturer specification.

Prevention — oil changes matter

The single most important thing you can do if you own a wet belt engine is change the oil on time — or early. Extended oil change intervals are the primary cause of wet belt degradation. If you're unsure of your vehicle's service history, book a diagnostic check immediately.
