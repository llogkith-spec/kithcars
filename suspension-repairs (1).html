Common faults

Suspension problems we fix.

South London roads are hard on suspension. These are the most common faults we see and fix.

Shock absorbers and struts

Worn shocks cause a bouncy, floaty ride and significantly increase stopping distances. We replace shocks and struts in axle pairs for balanced handling.

Coil springs

A broken coil spring causes knocking over bumps and a vehicle sitting unevenly — one corner visibly lower. A dangerous fault requiring immediate repair.

Wishbones and control arms

Worn wishbone bushes are one of the most common causes of front-end knocking — particularly common on South London roads. We replace wishbones and bushes as required.

Anti-roll bar links and bushes

Anti-roll bar drop links are a frequent cause of knocking over speed bumps. Inexpensive to replace — we carry stock of common sizes for same-day fitting.

Steering rack and track rod ends

Worn track rod ends cause vague steering and uneven tyre wear. We replace track rod ends and steering rack components as required.

Diagnostic approach

Before replacing parts, we carry out a proper inspection and road test. We won't recommend replacing parts unnecessarily — honest assessment, written quote before any work.
