# KITHCARS Website Brief

## Brand direction
- Trusted local vehicle repair specialist in Hackbridge and Wallington
- Clean, professional, and confident workshop experience
- Emphasis on quality workmanship, transparent pricing, and fast service
- Tone: reliable, direct, and customer-first

## Website goals
- Present KITHCARS as a trusted garage for servicing, bodywork, fleet support, and recovery
- Highlight key services with clear call-to-actions for quote requests
- Build confidence with proof of workmanship, warranty, and local availability
- Make contact details and hours easy to find

## Page structure
- Hero section with main headline and quote CTA
- Service categories: Servicing & Repairs, Bodywork & Customisation, Fleet, Recovery & Hire
- Dedicated service pages for Bodywork, Mechanical, and Fleet services
- Trust-paying sections for workmanship, pricing, and communication
- Recent work / gallery section
- Why choose us feature list
- Contact section with hours, address, phone, email, and enquiry form

## What I need from the client
- Workshop imagery and fleet photos
- Official logo file and brand color palette
- Any preferred page copy or FAQ details
- Specific service details or pricing notes
- Link to quote/registration page if you want it integrated

## Implementation notes
- Use static HTML/CSS/JS for fast loading and easy edits
- Keep the layout responsive and mobile-first
- Add local imagery after visual assets are provided
