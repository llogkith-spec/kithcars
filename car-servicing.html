Service types

Which service does your vehicle need?

Interim service — from £93

Recommended every 6 months or 6,000 miles. Includes engine oil and filter change, 30-point safety inspection covering brakes, tyres, lights, fluid levels, belts and more. Essential for high-mileage drivers or anyone using their vehicle for work.

Oil & filter service — from £131

Engine oil and filter change only — the fundamental service item that keeps your engine lubricated and protected. Suitable for newer vehicles on extended service intervals, or as an interim between full services.

Full service — from £141

Recommended annually or every 12,000 miles. Includes everything in the interim service plus air filter, cabin filter, brake fluid check, spark plug inspection (petrol), fuel filter check and a comprehensive 60-point inspection. Full written report provided.

Major service — from £176

The most comprehensive service — includes everything in the full service plus spark plug replacement, cambelt condition check, coolant flush and full system inspection. Recommended every 2 years or 24,000 miles, or when buying a used vehicle with incomplete service history.

Manufacturer schedule servicing

We follow the manufacturer's service schedule for your specific vehicle — whether that's a BMW, Audi, Mercedes, Ford, Vauxhall or any other make. Using quality branded parts that meet manufacturer specifications. Your service history is fully documented.

Full written inspection report

Every service includes a written inspection report covering all checked items — green for pass, amber for advisory, red for immediate attention required. You leave knowing exactly what condition your vehicle is in, with no surprises at the next MOT.
