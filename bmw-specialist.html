BMW Diagnostics

Timing Chain — N47 Engine

The BMW N47 diesel engine (1 Series, 3 Series, 5 Series) has a well-documented timing chain fault. Rattling on startup is the warning sign. We specialise in N47 timing chain replacement — this is the job we are known for.

Clutch Replacement — BMW

BMW clutches, including dual-mass flywheel replacements, across all models. 1 Series, 2 Series, 3 Series, 4 Series, 5 Series and more. Written quote before work starts.
