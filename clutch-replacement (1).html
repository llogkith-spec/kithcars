Clutch replacement — what you need to know.

A worn clutch doesn't always fail suddenly — these are the signs to watch for.

Pricing notice: All prices shown are estimates only. Final pricing depends on the specific parts required for your vehicle make, model and engine, and the actual labour time involved. Slipping clutch

Engine revs rising without the car accelerating, particularly uphill — the clutch plate is worn. The sooner it is replaced, the less damage to the flywheel.

Full clutch kit

We replace the complete kit — friction plate, pressure plate and release bearing. Never just the friction plate on its own.

Dual-mass flywheel

Many modern diesels require flywheel replacement alongside the clutch. Since the gearbox is already removed, doing both together saves significant labour cost.

Clutch hydraulic check

We inspect the clutch master and slave cylinders as part of every clutch replacement — a new clutch on failing hydraulics will feel wrong from day one.

Road tested before return

Every clutch replacement is followed by a full road test — smooth engagement, correct biting point, no shudder confirmed before handover.
