Common faults

Suspension problems we fix.

Shock absorbers and struts

Worn shock absorbers cause a bouncy, floaty ride and significantly increase stopping distances. A simple test: push down on each corner of the car — if it bounces more than once, the shock absorber is worn. We replace shocks and struts in axle pairs for balanced handling.

Coil springs

A broken coil spring is often felt as a knocking or clunking over bumps and a vehicle that sits unevenly — one corner visibly lower than the others. Broken springs are a dangerous fault and should be repaired immediately.

Wishbones and control arms

Worn wishbone bushes are one of the most common causes of front-end knocking. They deteriorate over time and are particularly prone on South London roads. We replace wishbones and bushes — pressed or full wishbone replacement as required.

Anti-roll bar links and bushes

Anti-roll bar drop links are a frequent cause of knocking — particularly over speed bumps or on rough surfaces. Inexpensive to replace and a common MOT failure item. We carry stock of common sizes for same-day fitting.

Steering rack and track rod ends

Worn track rod ends cause vague, wandering steering and uneven tyre wear. A clunking or knocking through the steering wheel, particularly on full lock, is a common symptom. We replace track rod ends and steering rack components as required.

Diagnostic approach

Before replacing parts, we carry out a proper visual inspection and road test to identify exactly what's worn and what isn't. We won't recommend replacing parts unnecessarily — you get an honest assessment and a clear written quote before any work starts.
